# Original-source text and visual audit

This file fixes the source-reading procedure used by the formalization.  A Lean
declaration is not credited as a formalization of a paper result merely because
its name or prose comment cites that result.

contains the absolute source path, the source SHA-256, the physical PDF page

The `pp153-186` pair is the default working text for the Theorem 3.11 to
Corollary 3.12 corridor.  Unlike the earlier 21-page extract, it retains pages
160-172, including Remarks 3.11.1-3.11.4 and the source explanations of IPL,
SHE, APT, and hidden internal structures.  The shorter extract is retained for
reproducibility, but must not be treated as a complete dependency text.

`plain` is the search index.  `layout` is used to locate displays and labels.
Neither is authoritative for a mathematical glyph.  The full layout extraction
of *Semi-graphs of Anabelioids* reported three uninterpretable-font warnings,
and both modes exhibit ligature, spacing, subscript, and decorated-symbol
damage.

The working order is deliberately asymmetric: use `plain`, then `layout`, then
the surrounding mathematical types and definitions while constructing Lean
interfaces.  Render and inspect the original page when the extracted variants
disagree, an interface cannot be typed coherently, a proof obligation fails for
what may be a variance/direction reason, or a declaration is about to receive
source-faithful credit.  This avoids repeatedly recognizing the same glyph
without allowing a plausible but weaker Lean statement to masquerade as the
paper's statement.  Stable ASCII identifiers should be used in Lean for
decorated paper symbols; comments and ledgers map them back to physical pages.

## Visually confirmed IUT III checkpoints

The physical PDF page numbers agree with the printed page numbers in this
edition.  Pages 153-159 and 173-186 were rendered and inspected.

| pages | source obligation confirmed from the original |
|---|---|
| 153-154 | Theorem 3.11 starts from initial Theta-data and a collection of distinct Hodge theaters.  Part (i) constructs procession-indexed topological modules, splitting monoids, number fields/Frobenioids, and then quotients this data by Ind1 and Ind2. |
| 155-156 | Part (ii) supplies the labelled log-Kummer correspondences.  Ind3 is explicitly upper-semi-compatible: it uses natural inclusions at nonarchimedean places and natural surjections at archimedean places, not equality. |
| 156-159 | Part (iii) requires compatibility of the Kummer isomorphisms and evaluation maps with horizontal LGP links, up to Ind1-Ind3.  The printed proof on p.159 sends these obligations back to the definitions and cited prior results; those references still have to be formalized. |
| 173-174 | Corollary 3.12 defines the procession-normalized holomorphic-hull volume `-|log(Theta)|` and the q-pilot volume `-|log(q)|` in different indeterminacy regimes.  Its stated comparison is `-|log(Theta)| >= -|log(q)|`. |
| 175-180 | Steps (i)-(x) fix one link, separate unit/value-group behavior, require simultaneous treatment, construct multiradial mono-analytic containers, and summarize the distinct roles of Ind1, Ind2, and Ind3. |
| 181 | Procession normalization makes log-volumes invariant under Ind1/Ind2 and converts Ind3 into an inequality from above.  The label average and tensor packets are part of this normalization. |
| 181-182 | Step (xi-b) requires the input prime-strip link (IPL), simultaneous holomorphic expressibility (SHE), and algorithmic parallel transport (APT). |
| 182-183 | Step (xi-c)/(xi-d) forms the holomorphic hull in the fixed 1-column arithmetic holomorphic structure and rewrites output using localizations of arithmetic vector bundles. |
| 183 | Comparability with the q-pilot arithmetic line bundle requires a suitable positive tensor power of the determinant and normalization over labels before applying log-volume.  The 1-column log-Kummer correspondence is stated to be crucial. |
| 184 | Step (xi-f) concludes `-|log(q)| in R_{<= -|log(Theta)|}`, hence `-|log(q)| <= -|log(Theta)|`.  This is the disputed membership/identification obligation, not a free ordered-real lemma. |
| 185 | Step (xi-h) explicitly warns that the argument does not routinely generalize to arbitrary `N >= 2` tensor powers of Theta-pilot objects; the corresponding sharper inequalities are stated to be false. |
| 186 | The proof ends after explaining why replacing the global realified Frobenioids by only local Frobenioids destroys a precise log-volume computation. |

Consequently, a generic determinant or tensor-product identity may be reused as
a lower algebraic kernel, but it cannot by itself discharge the source's Step
(xi) normalization.

## Theorem-contract and admission rule

Formalization is statement-first.  Before an upstream declaration is consumed
by the next layer, its contract records the source locator, quantified objects,
hypotheses, conclusion, variance/direction, and scope restrictions.  The Lean
statement need not be syntactically identical to the paper, but any different
carrier or formulation requires a proved equivalence/realization bridge.  A
strictly weaker consequence does not satisfy the contract.

Proof provenance is recorded independently of statement fidelity:

- `paper-route`: the Lean proof follows the source's cited construction;
- `alternative-route`: Lean proves the same theorem contract by another route,
  with the difference documented;
- `interface-only`: the contract is represented, but its mathematical content
  is still supplied through a field, premise, axiom, or `sorry`.

An alternative proof is acceptable and may be used by higher layers once the
contract and carrier bridges are checked.  It must not be described as a
formalization of the paper's proof method.

A source result is marked `proved` only after all four checks pass:

1. the original statement, hypotheses, objects, and variance directions are
   mapped to explicit Lean types;
2. any alternative carrier has a proved equivalence/realization bridge to the
   source carrier;
3. the relevant theorem is constructed rather than supplied as a record field,
   arbitrary premise, empty carrier, or `sorry`;
4. the target module builds on the current toolchain and its `#print axioms`
   result is recorded.
